## Method

- Topic = case-insensitive regex on the title (scripts/04_topics.py). Precision was checked by reading random matched titles (results/precision_review.md); recall is not measured (papers that do not name the topic in the title are missed).
- Per topic: logistic regression topic ~ venue + year on papers 2024-2026, so changes are within venue (2026 rests on ICRA, RSS and the journals). Annual multiplier = linear-in-year version. Benjamini-Hochberg over topics; 'rising'/'declining' need q < 0.05, otherwise 'unclear'.
- Year = publication/conference year; conference papers were written 6-9 months earlier.
