# Robotics topics at top venues, 2020–2026: share and change (title + abstract)

Generated by scripts/15_report.py from results/shares_and_change_ta.csv.

**Measure.** A paper counts for a topic when its own work (method, system, task, platform, data or evaluation) involves the
topic, as read from its title and abstract by a language model (Claude Opus 5.5) against the codebook
data/semantic_codebook_v2.md (labels: data/semantic_labels.csv; how they were made: data/semantic_labelling_prompt.md;
checks: results/semantic_validation.md). Until 2026-10-28 topics were keyword rules; that matrix is kept as
data/topic_matrix_ta_keyword.csv (results/shares_and_change_ta_keyword.csv, precision checks results/precision_review.md).
**Papers** = research articles only (editorials, commentaries, Focus/highlight pieces, errata, special-issue introductions
and reviews/surveys removed: scripts/03b_research_articles.py, list in results/removed_nonresearch.txt; KEEP_REVIEWS=False).
**Share** = percentage of papers, standardised to the 2025 venue mix of all seven venues so that the years compare;
2026 has no IROS yet, so its share is the 2025 mix moved by the 2026 within-venue change at ICRA, RSS, CoRL and the
journals (95% intervals in results/shares_and_change_ta.csv). **CoRL 2026** is the accepted-papers list of the conference site (no PMLR volume yet); its abstracts are those of the papers' arXiv versions, found for 575 of 687 papers; the other 112 have no abstract and are not counted. **Change** = first to last year in percentage points;
**per year** = annual odds multiplier over the whole period from topic ~ venue + year (95% CI), **recent** = the same
over 2024–2026 (↑/↓ when significant); **trend** uses the whole period, Benjamini–Hochberg q < 0.05.

## Reading notes

- Shares overlap: a paper can count for several topics (e.g. a VLA paper is usually also manipulation and imitation learning).
- 'Manipulation (any)' is a broad umbrella, not a research topic.
- A share counts papers whose own work involves the topic; a topic named only as background, motivation, contrast or future work does not count. It is not a count of papers whose main contribution is the topic (those labels are the "main" column of data/semantic_labels.csv; their table: results/shares_and_change_ta_main.csv).
- RSS and CoRL have 74–575 papers a year; ICRA and IROS dominate the totals.
- The labels are one model's reading. A second labelling of a random 10% agrees closely (results/semantic_validation.md),
  but that shows consistency, not correctness; the only check of correctness so far is a re-judgement by the same model,
  which is not independent, and no human check has been made.
- Topics per paper rise over the years (2020: 3.05, 2021: 3.06, 2022: 3.17, 2023: 3.27, 2024: 3.43, 2025: 3.64, 2026: 4.24), and abstracts got
  longer (median 2020: 164, 2021: 169, 2022: 170, 2023: 174, 2024: 173, 2025: 173, 2026: 178 words): part of a small rise can come from
  papers describing more of their work; this does not explain the large risers (LLM/VLM, diffusion, VLA, imitation learning).
