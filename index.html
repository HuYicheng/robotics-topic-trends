    – papers

      Papers

      Accepted papers of ICRA, IROS, RSS and CoRL, and articles of T-RO, IJRR and Science Robotics, 2024 to October 2026, that have an abstract (11,017 of 11,135; the missing ones are mostly Science Robotics editorials and commentaries). Sources: the dblp XML dump of 2026-10-13 (IROS, journals), OpenAlex (ICRA), the RSS proceedings site, PMLR (CoRL). Each source was checked against dblp on a year both cover.

      Topics

      Each topic is a written rule (keywords and phrases). A paper counts for a topic when the rule matches its title or its abstract. Words that are generic inside abstracts (dataset, safety, mapping, detection, uncertainty, mechanism, reasoning) have stricter abstract rules. For every topic, random papers matched only through the abstract were read to check that the word is used in the topic's sense; rules were corrected until the samples held up.

      Shares and trends

      Shares are standardised to the 2025 mix of the seven venues, so the three years compare. 2026 has no IROS or CoRL yet; its share applies the within-venue 2026 change seen at ICRA, RSS and the journals, with a 95% interval. The trend is a logistic regression of topic on venue and year; a topic is called rising or declining only when significant after Benjamini–Hochberg correction across the 59 topics.

      Limits

      • A share counts papers that name a topic, including in passing; it is not a count of papers whose main contribution is that topic.
      • Keyword rules miss papers that describe a topic without its usual words.
      • RSS and CoRL publish 130–260 papers a year, so their numbers move in steps of about half a point.
      • Conference years follow submission by six to nine months; arXiv runs ahead of all of this.
